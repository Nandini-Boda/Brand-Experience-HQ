# Dashboard Modules
## Hospitality Weekly Insight Hub

### 1. Overview

Hospitality Weekly Insight Hub is organized into five primary dashboard sections. Each section focuses on a different aspect of hospitality business performance.

This document describes the purpose and reporting focus of each module.

### 2. Revenue

**Purpose:** Provide visibility into sales and revenue performance.

Key reporting areas:

- Food and beverage sales
- Revenue and sales targets
- Item-level sales performance
- Sales booking information
- Upselling performance
- Upselling target comparisons

**Business Value:** Helps users review sales performance, compare results with targets and understand additional revenue opportunities.

### 3. Brand

**Purpose:** Provide visibility into brand performance and guest experience.

Key reporting areas:

- Guest ratings
- Guest reviews
- Brand-level performance
- Guest feedback trends

**Business Value:** Helps users understand guest feedback and monitor brand experience indicators.

### 4. People

**Purpose:** Present workforce-related information and training activity.

Key reporting areas:

- Workforce information
- Training attendance
- Employee training participation
- People-related reporting indicators

**Business Value:** Supports workforce visibility and monitoring of employee learning and development activity.

### 5. Property

**Purpose:** Provide visibility into property-level operational information.

Key reporting areas:

- Guest complaints
- Complaint categories
- Property-related operational indicators
- Guest issue monitoring

**Business Value:** Helps identify operational concerns and areas that may require attention.

### 6. Executive Review

**Purpose:** Bring important management discussion points into a structured review environment.

Key reporting areas:

- Executive discussion items
- Management review topics
- Follow-up information
- Action status monitoring

**Business Value:** Supports structured management discussions and visibility into outstanding follow-up items.

### 7. Integrated Reporting Experience

The five modules are designed to complement one another.

Together, they provide a consolidated view of hospitality performance across:

- Financial and sales performance
- Brand and guest experience
- Workforce and training
- Property operations
- Management review and follow-up

### 8. Summary

Hospitality Weekly Insight Hub brings multiple business reporting perspectives into one interactive dashboard.

Its modular structure helps users navigate between business areas while maintaining a consistent reporting experience.
